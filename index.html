Academic Profile

Computer Science faculty member with experience in teaching, research, AI applications, GIS systems and web-based academic tools. My PhD work at IFTM University uses deep learning on satellite data to map and monitor permafrost.

Permafrost Probability Mapping

Machine learning and deep learning models that map permafrost in the Western Himalaya’s Jhelum Basin from satellite data.

Ongoing (PhD)

Multi-Modal SAR + Optical Fusion

PermaFusionNet: a hybrid deep learning framework that combines SAR and optical imagery to map permafrost degradation.
