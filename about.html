ABOUT ME

Dedication to Computational Excellence

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Moradabad Institute of Technology (MIT), Moradabad, with over 13 years of experience across universities, engineering colleges, schools and the banking sector.

I am pursuing my Ph.D. at IFTM University, Moradabad, on “Deep Learning for Permafrost Monitoring using Remote Sensing Data”, under the supervision of Dr. Sunil Kumar and Dr. Rakesh Sahu (Bennett University). My research applies machine learning and deep learning to satellite imagery to map permafrost in the Western Himalaya.

I mentor final-year B.Tech projects, publish with my students, review for international conferences, and lead the KAPILA / Patent / IPR Cell at MIT Moradabad.

Research Interests

Artificial Intelligence Machine Learning Deep Learning Remote Sensing Permafrost Mapping Computer Vision GIS & Geospatial Analysis IoT Cyber Security
