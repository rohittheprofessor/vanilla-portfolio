RESEARCH

Academic Research Work

My PhD research uses deep learning on multi-sensor satellite data to map and monitor permafrost in the Himalaya. Alongside it, I supervise applied AI and IoT projects with my students.

Permafrost Mapping using Deep Learning

Deep learning models that map permafrost in the Western Himalaya using remote sensing data and geospatial analysis. Part of my PhD at IFTM University.

Ongoing (PhD Work)

Comparative ML for Permafrost Probability

Compares machine learning approaches to permafrost probability mapping in the Jhelum Basin. Presented at IEEE ISED-2025, NIT Raipur.

Published · IEEE Xplore

PermaFusionNet: SAR + Optical Fusion

A hybrid deep learning framework that fuses SAR and optical data to map permafrost degradation. Presented at IIRA 5.0 – 2026.

Published · IJERT

Deep Learning for Satellite Image Processing: A Review

A critical review of deep learning methods for satellite image processing and permafrost probability mapping, with future directions. Presented at IEEE IC2PCT-2026.

Published · IEEE Xplore

Machine Learning on Geospatial Data

Supervised and deep learning techniques for analysing large geospatial datasets for prediction and classification.

Ongoing

Cyber Security & IoT

Book chapters on social engineering, social media security, Edge AI for IoT, and IoT for sustainable agriculture.
